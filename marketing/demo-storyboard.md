## Production Notes

### Screen Recording Tips
- Use a clean Enso installation (no clutter in sidebar)
- Set browser to 1920×1080 with 100% zoom
- Hide browser toolbar/bookmarks for cleaner shots
- Use a dark desktop wallpaper (matches Enso's dark theme)
- Record at 60fps, export at 30fps for smooth playback

### Music
- Lo-fi beats or ambient electronic (no lyrics)
- Build tempo slightly through scenes 2-4, resolve in scene 6
- Recommended: Artlist or Epidemic Sound royalty-free tracks

### Text Overlays
- Font: Inter or system-ui (matches Enso's UI)
- Color: #e5e7eb (light gray on dark)
- Accent color: #818cf8 (indigo, matches Enso's brand)
- Animation: Simple fade-in, no flashy transitions

### Thumbnail
- Use the OG image (1200×630) as the video thumbnail
- Alt: Screenshot of the research board with "14 seconds" callout
