Three Pillars

What makes Enso fundamentally different from every other AI tool.

🏭

Own the Factory

Self-hosted. Open source. Your code, your data, your API keys. No subscriptions. No vendor lock-in. During setup, Claude Code personalizes the entire app based on who you are — a developer gets "Forge," an investor gets "Signal," a researcher gets "Nexus."

📱

Every Answer Is an App

Research questions become interactive dashboards with 48+ sources. Build requests become running apps in a live terminal. File operations become a desktop-grade file manager. 15+ built-in app types, plus custom apps from a single command.

🧬

AI That Evolves Itself

Type /evolve and an AI team — project leader, architect, engineers, QA — runs a full improvement sprint on your installation. Sprint scores went from 4.5 to 7.5 over 4 cycles. No other developer tool does this.

How It Works

From zero to running in three steps.

1

Ask

Type any question or goal. The task router auto-classifies the complexity and picks the right approach — simple chat, Claude Code, or full multi-agent orchestration.

2

Build

Watch it work. Research boards assemble in 14 seconds. Apps build in a live terminal. Multi-agent teams coordinate on dependency graphs. Every result is an interactive app.

3

Evolve

Type /evolve and an AI team autonomously improves your entire installation — apps, prompts, UI, everything. Each sprint makes it better, customized to you.
