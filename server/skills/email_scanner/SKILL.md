# Email Scanner

Email Scanner: analyze email communication patterns — top contacts, recent subjects, and correspondence insights from Outlook or IMAP.

## Available Tools

### enso_email_scannerbrowse (primary)

Browse your email contacts, top correspondents, and recent email subjects.

Parameters:
- `query` (string): Filter contacts or subjects

### enso_email_scannerscan

Scan your email inbox for communication patterns.

Parameters:
- `folder` (string): Email folder to scan (default: INBOX)
- `limit` (number): Max emails to scan (default: 50)
